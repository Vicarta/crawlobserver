## Root Cause

The target session published terminal `completed` at `10:37:13`, Quality Gate
persisted 20 zero-PageRank top pages at `10:37:18`, and the PageRank mutation
became visible at `10:37:19`. The old quality row therefore described an earlier
storage revision than the PageRank report. Heuristic refresh and unversioned
Current Snapshot promotion could preserve or replay that mismatch.

## Implemented Contract

- PageRank computation publishes started/finalized/failed evidence and terminal
  completion waits for finalized verified evidence.
- PageRank reports and quality metrics share `pagerank-eligible-v1` and fail
  closed against the newest lifecycle event.
- Quality evaluations/findings are immutable and addressed by deterministic
  revision; current, action, and promotion state have separate durable pointers
  or append-only histories.
- Current Snapshot uses strict historical baselines and a monotonic source and
  content watermark, including restart, fold, compaction, and Delta replay.
- Re-evaluation is admin-only, confirmed, audited, idempotent, and never starts
  a crawl or PageRank computation.
- UI provenance and repair state are refreshed from authoritative GET results.
