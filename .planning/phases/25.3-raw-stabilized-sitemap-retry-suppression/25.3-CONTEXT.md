# Phase 25.3: Raw-Stabilized Sitemap Retry Suppression - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-08-28
**Status:** Ready for execution
**Source:** User correction, production read-only audit, and Phase 25.2 contract

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Fix the Phase 25.2 execution gap that repeatedly schedules every sitemap
lastmod difference still ahead of Published Current Snapshot, even after two
completed raw Delta observations prove that the same URL+lastmod cohort is
content-stable.

This phase changes candidate execution, API/UI
provenance, tests, and documentation. It does not alter the Phase 26 sitemap
report, lower saved project ceilings, promote untrusted raw evidence, repair
historical sessions, or run a live crawl/rescan.
</domain>

<decisions>
## Locked Decisions

### Publication and execution are separate authorities

- Current Snapshot remains the sole published comparison, reporting, quality,
  and promotion authority.
- Raw stability may suppress a redundant HTTP refetch. It cannot advance
  sitemap publication lineage, copy raw pages into Current Snapshot, satisfy a
  quality gate, or make a slim canary run publication-eligible.
- Preview must show both the number of URLs ahead of Published Snapshot and the
  smaller number requiring network execution.

### Stability proof is per URL and conservative

- Require two distinct completed, non-synthetic, project-bound fresh sitemap
  observations ordered deterministically by `(finished_at, session_id)`.
- The same normalized URL must carry the same valid lastmod in both observations.
- Both sessions must contain terminal page evidence for that URL and the same
  nonzero main-content hash. Missing/error/zero-hash evidence never proves
  stability.
- A fresh tuple matching that proof becomes `sitemap_stable_unpublished` and is
  not launched solely for lastmod. Any newly forward lastmod, missing proof,
  failed attempt, or changed content remains actionable.
- Derive proof read-only from immutable ClickHouse rows. Do not introduce an
  acknowledgement mutation or backfill table in this urgent phase.

### Candidate limits remain ceilings, not targets

- Do not migrate DI or any other project to an arbitrary 50-80 candidate
  target. `max_candidates_per_run`, changed, new, and discovery limits remain
  upper safety ceilings.
- When a sitemap event is new or genuinely changed and no conservative
  stability proof exists, Daily Delta may legitimately scan most or all of the
  site up to those configured ceilings.
- Suppression is permitted only for the exact redundant retry proven by the
  two-observation contract above; candidate volume alone is never a reason to
  discard work.

### Legacy bootstrap is explicit

- The DI Aug 27/Aug 28 sessions predate selector v2 but contain completed fresh
  sitemap refreshes and page evidence. They may form a `legacy_complete_pair`
  only through the same exact per-URL proof.
- A legacy label/config alone never proves stability. Running, stopped, failed,
  partial, cross-project, or mismatched-origin evidence is rejected.

### Selector v2 and publication hold

- Classify differences against Published Snapshot first. Persist published
  difference total, actionable total, stable acknowledged total, selected,
  deferred, canaries, proof lineage/digest, and source per URL.
- Existing changed/new/global limits remain ceilings for actionable work.
- Stable acknowledgement is not ordinary `unchanged`, canary, or deferred work.
- Persist an explicit publication-ineligible/held flag while any Published
  difference is acknowledged only by raw stability.

### Conditional validators

- Quality, analytic, and conditional-request baselines remain Current Snapshot.
- Do not use a newer raw ETag/Last-Modified for a launched URL: the current
  overlay intentionally skips `304` rows, so a raw validator could otherwise
  retain a stale Current Snapshot representation.
- Existing Current Snapshot-bound conditional GET and `304` behavior remains
  unchanged. A future raw-validator feature requires an exact per-URL overlay
  source binding and is outside this urgent phase.

### Safety and documentation

- Update `ProductFeatures.md` in the functional change.
- Use fixtures/fakes for tests; no production crawl/rescan or historical data
  rewrite.
- Deploy app-only through the active-crawl-blocking safe gate, never force, and
  use read-only DI Preview plus health/ClickHouse/log checks for acceptance.
</decisions>

## Production Case

- Published Current Snapshot: `2aa4424f-fe2c-460e-8dc0-fdbd494f5079`, revision 1.
- Consecutive completed Delta sessions:
  `bef21482-4513-486d-8999-1374f25daa9e` and
  `636f285a-743a-4c4d-aca3-cde03eaf08f9`.
- Fresh sitemap: 2,088 URLs; 2,059 forward lastmods and 4 added relative to
  Published Snapshot.
- Bulk-reset signature: 1,858 URLs at `2026-08-15T16:53:17Z` and 103 at
  `2026-08-15T16:43:31Z`.
- The two Delta sessions have identical content hashes, status, title, and H1
  for all 2,094 joined pages; 2,090 ETags and Last-Modified values are also
  identical.
- Promotion was blocked by one unmatched candidate (`2,087/2,088`), leaving the
  stale Published Snapshot in place and causing perpetual replay.
</specifics>
